## 1. INTRODUCTION AND METHODOLOGICAL JUSTIFICATION OF SAMPLING

This research adopted a **Progressive Multi-Stage Sampling and Geographic Stratification Strategy**, designed to test the hypothesis of consistency in the computer processing of electoral data.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 1: USA (N=987)       ──>  PHASE 2: SPAIN (N=696)    ──>  PHASE 3: NATIONAL (N=233,448)|
| (Primary Diaspora Sample)       (Homologous Replication)       (Aggregated Baseline)  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1.1 Investigation Origin and Cognitive Lens (ADHD Pattern Recognition)
The entire research originated on **June 1, 2026**, through the meticulous observation of an initial consulate (Los Angeles). Driven by the cognitive capacity for **intense hyperfocus and pattern recognition** (associated with the researcher's neurodivergent ADHD profile), a subtle graphical and syntactic anomaly was identified that would have otherwise gone unnoticed under standard review.

Systematically following that initial lead, the investigation evolved from the manual analysis of a single consular tally sheet to the development of **automated web scraping and massive audit Python scripts** (`muestreo_masivo_deepfakes.py`, `auditoria_masiva_xref.sh`), capable of downloading and processing all 117,993 PDF files nationwide.

### 1.2 Why Start with the United States ($N = 987$ tally sheets)?
The United States represents the abroad electoral district with the largest volume of registered voters and installed polling stations ($N = 987$). Methodologically, it offered three ideal baseline conditions:
1. **Logistical Homogeneity:** Operation under an advance voting scheme (Monday through Sunday) with centralized consular scanning and transmission.
2. **Larger Initial Sample Size:** Allowed establishing a variance baseline without the dispersed noise of isolated rural stations.
3. **Initial Finding:** Structural analysis revealed that $100\%$ of the PDF files presented syntactic inconsistencies in the cross-reference table (`xref`) and metadata purging (`Creator`, `Producer`, `CreationDate`).

### 1.2 Sample Replication in Spain ($N = 696$ tally sheets): Statistically Homologous Samples
To determine whether the phenomenon detected in the US was due to a local anomaly (an isolated scanner or server) or a systemic pattern, **Spain ($N = 696$ tally sheets)** was selected as a replication group.

* **Statistical Homology Criterion:** Spain constitutes the second most populous abroad electoral district and shares equivalent socio-electoral characteristics with the US diaspora (advance voting patterns, consular registry distributions, and remote transmission).
* **Replication Result:** The Spain sample presented the exact same structural footprint as the US sample ($100\%$ XREF inconsistencies and metadata omission). This statistically validated that the phenomenon responded to a **centralized document processing pipeline for abroad voting**, ruling out the hypothesis of an accidental technical error at a specific consular site.

### 1.3 Scaling to the National Baseline ($N = 233,448$ polling stations)
After confirming the pattern in the two most representative abroad samples, the research scaled to the entire national consolidation universe ($N = 233,448$ stations), allowing a comparison between diaspora behavior (Department 88) and national territory behavior (32 departments).

## 2. STATISTICAL FREQUENCY ANALYSIS (BENFORD'S SECOND DIGIT LAW - 2BL)

Benford's Second Digit Law (2BL) establishes the expected probabilistic frequency for the second numerical position in dataset values generated by organic or natural processes.

### 2.1 Theoretical Mathematical Model
The theoretical probability $P(d)$ of the second digit being $d \in \{0, 1, \dots, 9\}$ is given by:
$$P(d) = \sum_{k=1}^{9} \log_{10} \left( 1 + \frac{1}{10k + d} \right)$$

## 4. DISCUSSION AND CONTEXT ANALYSIS

1. **Abroad Context and Multiculturality (Diaspora):** 
   In the US and Spain consulates, voting behavior is strongly mediated by **heterogeneous sociocultural and demographic factors** (length of residency, economic integration levels, regional origins in Colombia, and migrant community mobilization dynamics). These socio-behavioral conditions introduce natural variations in turnout and vote concentration. However, the aggregated 2BL test for Department 88 revealed a deviation exceeding sociocultural variance ($Z = +3.53$ on digit `1` and $Z = -3.81$ on digit `3`).

2. **National Context (Colombia):** 
   At the national level ($N > 229,000$ stations), the $\chi^2$ tests for both candidates far exceed the distribution critical values. The presence of Z-Scores of $-20.98$ and $-27.31$ in high digits ($8$ and $9$) demonstrates an overrepresentation of low digits ($0, 1, 2$), a phenomenon documented in data auditing literature as an indicator of quantitative truncation or shaping.

3. **Forensic Limitation:** Under **ISO/IEC 27037** and **NIST SP 800-86** standards, statistical tests constitute **atypicality indicators for audit prioritization**, requiring direct verification of database transaction logs and physical confrontation against original paper forms.
