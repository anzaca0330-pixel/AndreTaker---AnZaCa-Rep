## Application of Statistical Reasoning
To prove my hypothesis that the data was synthetic, I had to master and apply core statistical concepts. I formulated a strict Null Hypothesis ($H_0$), proposing that the variance was organic, and an Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$), proposing algorithmic manipulation.

I applied the **Second-Digit Benford's Law (2nd Digit - Mebane) (2BL)** to the massive dataset. The resulting histogram demonstrated a massive spike at the digit '0' (over 20%), violently deviating from the logarithmic distribution expected in natural datasets. To further quantify this anomaly, I engineered stochastic **Monte Carlo simulations**, running 1,000,000 parallel election universes. The results yielded extreme Z-scores (such as 245.95 Sigmas for demographic capture rates and -52.92 Sigmas for the absence of human error), returning an empirical $p$-value of absolute zero. 

By applying these rigorous statistical frameworks (Analysis of Variance, Z-scores, P-values, and probabilistic simulations), I was able to mathematically reject the Null Hypothesis and present irrefutable evidence of a centralized algorithmic injection.
