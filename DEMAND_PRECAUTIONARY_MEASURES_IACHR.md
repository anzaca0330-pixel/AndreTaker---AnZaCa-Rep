### III. SYNTHESIS OF FORENSIC EVIDENTIARY RECORD

The evidence underpinning this demand is technical-scientific, irrefutable, and freely accessible for international audit (Peer Review). It is divided into two proven attack vectors:

#### A. Physical-Mathematical Fraud ("Ironing") and Benford's Law
Our audit applied Benford's Law (second-digit analysis or 2BL) to the national results. The results demonstrate a statistically impossible deviation in the vote distribution in favor of Abelardo de la Espriella (with massive anomalous spikes in digits 8 and 9). We detected the phenomenon of "ironing" (planchado), where entire blocks of contiguous polling stations present near-zero variance, which is the indisputable mathematical signature of human (invented) assignment of results, overriding the organic behavior of the electorate.

#### B. Cybernetic Fraud ("Template B" and the Pixel Paradox)
The definitive proof of documentary impersonation lies in the structural analysis of the E-14 Forms. 
- **The Injection:** The official PDFs host intruding graphic vectors (`DeviceGray`) that obfuscate and overwrite the vote information.
- **The Structural "Scar":** Using binary metrology tools (`qpdf`), we demonstrated that both the Pre-count files (Delegates) and Scrutiny files (Claveros) share an identical structural damage in their cross-reference table (XREF): *`reported number of objects (15) is not one plus the highest object number (13)`*. 
- **Break in the Chain of Custody:** Given that the Claveros documents must legally be independent digitizations of physical paper sheets extracted from security bags days later, it is computationally impossible for them to share the exact same underlying code injection as the web-based Delegates files. This proves that **the Claveros documents in the official database are synthetic clones**, not legitimate scans.
