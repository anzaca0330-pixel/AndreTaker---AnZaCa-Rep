Learning Outcome 1: Apply principles of probability and statistical distribution

Describe the specific experience(s) that demonstrate you have met this learning outcome.

Experience Description: When analyzing the initial data from the Los Angeles polling stations, I identified a highly irregular, flat distribution of data. To determine if this was a natural occurrence, I had to apply principles of statistical distribution. I utilized the Second-Digit Benford's Law (2nd Digit - Mebane) (2BL) to compare the observed distribution of the data against the logarithmic distribution expected in natural human behavior. The resulting histogram showed a massive spike at the digit '0', which violated the expected probability.

Furthermore, I engineered a Monte Carlo stochastic model, generating 1,000,000 simulated parallel universes to calculate the empirical probability ($p$-value) of observing a 98.18% demographic capture rate. The simulation proved that such a distribution sat at an impossible 245.95 standard deviations (Sigmas) from the mean.

Learning Outcome 2: Interpret measures of central tendency, variance, and standard deviation

Describe the specific experience(s) that demonstrate you have met this learning outcome.

Experience Description: The catalyst of my investigation was the direct calculation and interpretation of variance and standard deviation ($\sigma$). In reviewing five independent data clusters (Tables 001-005), I calculated a standard deviation of merely 2.5 on a base of 80 data points per cluster. I successfully interpreted that this variance was 5.9 times lower than the expected organic variance.

By calculating the mean ($\mu$) and standard deviation across the national dataset, I was able to isolate extreme outliers using an Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) scatter plot. I interpreted these near-zero standard deviations as absolute proof of a fixed algorithmic assignment (a Pseudo-Random Number Generator) overriding natural variance, proving my proficiency in interpreting these statistical metrics.

Learning Outcome 3: Formulate and test hypotheses using statistical models

Describe the specific experience(s) that demonstrate you have met this learning outcome.

Experience Description: The entirety of the forensic audit was structured around rigorous hypothesis testing. I defined a strict Null Hypothesis ($H_0$): The data distribution across all polling stations is organic, and any observed variance falls within expected natural sociological parameters. My Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$) was that the data exhibited artificial suppression of variance indicating synthetic generation.

To test this, I utilized the results from the Benford's Law (2nd Digit - Mebane) analysis and the Monte Carlo simulations. The calculated $p$-value from the simulations was $p = 0.0$ (due to events sitting at -52.92 and 245.95 Sigmas). Based on these statistical models, I mathematically rejected the Null Hypothesis, concluding that the data was synthetic. This real-world application demonstrates my ability to formulate, test, and draw conclusions from hypotheses using advanced statistical methods.
